Typography

Poppins, one family

Weights 300, 400, 500, 600 and 700. Headings use 400 in navy. Emphasis inside a heading uses 600.

Heading one 64px

Heading two 44px

Heading three 36px

Eyebrow label, 600 uppercase

Lead paragraph at 20px with a 31px line height. Short sentences, plain English.

Body paragraph at 16px with a 25px line height. Line length stays under about 80 characters. Strong text uses 600.

Caption in muted. Use with care, see the accessibility table.

Accessibility

Contrast checks

WCAG contrast ratios. AA needs 4.5:1 for normal text and 3:1 for large text (18px or 14px bold and up).

Three values need a decision. Muted captions fail on white. Error text fails at normal size. White on sea green fails at normal size. These match the site's own values, so this page does not change them. Settle them before the demo.
